# Sortiva

**Your Files. Perfectly Organized.**

Sortiva is a Windows desktop app that keeps your folders tidy. It sorts files by type and date, reads the real shooting date from photos and videos, finds duplicates, cleans up old installers, and can organize on a schedule.

> 🎁 **Free account + 30 days of Pro** — Install Sortiva, open **Pro & account** and create a free account with email or GitHub. Every new account gets **30 days of Pro at no cost**; when the trial ends the app keeps working on the free plan. No card required.

## Download

Get the latest installer from the [Releases](https://github.com/omidmasumi740-dev/sortiva/releases/latest) page — or open the app website at https://omidmasumi740-dev.github.io/sortiva/ — and run it. Existing settings, history, the account session and an activated Pro license are kept across upgrades.

After installing: open **Pro & account** → **Create account** (email or GitHub) → your 30-day Pro trial starts immediately.

## 🆓 Free plan

The free plan is a complete organizer that never expires:

- Organize by type and date — Simple, Images, Videos, Documents, Archives, Music and Software pages with every grouping option (Movies & Series, photo dates or EXIF GPS location, Artist / Album, format, year…)
- Jalali (شمسی) and Gregorian folders, real EXIF / video shooting dates, cross-drive moves with progress and Cancel, long-path support
- Preview before moving, with rule-overlap, target-collision and existing-file warnings
- Auto Organize while the app is open (folders, interval, Simple structure) plus **Run Now**
- Full history with undo; CSV/JSON **export with the free account**
- Light / Dark / System themes, 8 accent colors, UI scale, English and Persian RTL

## ⭐ Pro

Pro adds smart rules & presets, the duplicate finder, daily Windows Task Scheduler runs (even when the app is closed), folder watch, a custom accent color, installer & Temp cleanup, and the advanced auto-organize structures. A new account tries all of it free for 30 days.

## Highlights

- One-click organization by type and date (Jalali and Gregorian folders)
- Auto Organize with interval or daily schedule, folder watch and Task Scheduler support
- Rules engine with content/OCR conditions, destination and rename templates
- Duplicate finder and installer/Temp cleanup with Recycle Bin safety
- Preview with conflict warnings, full history with undo, CSV/JSON export
- English and Persian interface, built-in updater
- One plan per email and computer — the free trial is granted once per account, email, GitHub identity and machine
